Why it works for the brand

  • Next to the right neighboursWestside, BIBA, Peter England, Basics and Maybell trade within 500 m on the same road, so the shopper already here is buying apparel.
  • A shopfront in line with the streetAt 12 ft, the frontage sits close to the 13 to 17 ft that most brands on this stretch work with.
  • Depth for the full rangeA 73 ft run lets apparel lead at the front with home and lifestyle further in, and the mezzanine adds a second zone.
  • Ready nowWarm shell with power backup and immediate possession shortens the fit out timeline.
